## Completion criterion

Source completeness here means the retained displayed/named statements and the independently inventoried substantive assertions are proved under the source definitions. It does not mean word-for-word translation of all proof prose. An equivalent proof may replace intermediate steps, but cannot replace the stated result with a weaker assertion or with an unconnected auxiliary quantity. `SemanticDependencies.lean` checks actual proof-term paths, and the scoped source reviews inspect mathematical correspondence. The clean build and axiom audit are separate validation gates.
