# Pure-density-matrix Krylov complexity dominates twice spread complexity

## Theorem and assumptions

Let G be Hermitian and psi a unit vector. Define spread complexity using the state-vector Lanczos basis generated by (G,psi). Define operator complexity using the Hilbert–Schmidt-normalized rank-one density matrix R=|psi><psi| and [G,.]. Then, for all real t,
\[
\boxed{C_K(R(t))\ge2C_S(\psi(t)).}
\]
This holds in every finite dimension, including degenerate spectra, stationary vectors and rank-deficient original density matrices whose purifications are being studied. It also holds for infinite-dimensional systems whenever the standard normalized Lanczos expansions used by the source exist: the evolved vectors lie in the relevant closed polynomial Krylov spaces. The nonnegative-sum proof allows infinite complexities as extended nonnegative values. No finite cutoff is promoted to an infinite-dimensional theorem. For unbounded generators without the domains/completeness needed to define those normalized expansions, this theorem does not assign a complexity that the source has not defined.

## Proof by nested polynomial flags

Let K_i be the orthonormal state Krylov basis. In row vectorization,
\[
\operatorname{vec}R=\psi\otimes\bar\psi,\qquad
L=G\otimes I-I\otimes\bar G.
\]
The vectorized evolution is chi(t)=psi(t) tensor conjugate(psi(t)). The Krylov basis for (-conjugate(G),conjugate(psi)) is (-1)^i conjugate(K_i); the sign has no effect on probabilities or on the spans below.

Define the joint Krylov flag
\[
V_n=\operatorname{span}\{L^k(\psi\otimes\bar\psi):0\le k\le n\},
\]
and the total-degree product flag
\[
W_n=\operatorname{span}\{K_i\otimes\bar K_j:i+j\le n\}.
\]
The binomial expansion of L^k shows V_n is contained in W_n. This remains true when either chain terminates or the joint cyclic subspace is a proper subspace of the tensor product. No basis completion is needed.

For nested orthogonal projections, P_Wn-P_Vn is an orthogonal projection and is positive semidefinite. If p_k^op is the operator Krylov distribution and p_i is the state distribution, then
\[
\sum_{k>n}p_k^{op}
=1-\|P_{V_n}\chi(t)\|^2
\ge1-\|P_{W_n}\chi(t)\|^2
=\sum_{i+j>n}p_i p_j.
\]
Thus the operator Krylov index stochastically dominates the sum of two independent indices having the state's Krylov distribution. Summing over n>=0 gives
\[
C_K(R(t))\ge\sum_{i,j}(i+j)p_i p_j=2C_S(\psi(t)).
\]
All terms are nonnegative, so the same step is justified by monotone convergence/Tonelli in an admissible infinite chain. When both sides are finite there is the exact gap identity
\[
C_K-2C_S=\sum_{n\ge0}\|(P_{W_n}-P_{V_n})\chi(t)\|^2.
\]
For divergent quantities use the preceding tail inequalities, not an undefined infinity-minus-infinity expression.

## Separate application to each required branch

**TARGET 3-I.** Use the actual normalized initial purification s=|Psi_rho>, G_I=H tensor I, state seed s and operator seed s s^dagger. The theorem proves CK(Psi_I)>=2 CS(Psi_I). In full-rank canonical notation s=vec(sqrt(rho)); the proof does not require full rank or this coordinate representation.

**TARGET 3-U*.** Use the actual normalized initial purification s, G_U=H tensor I-I tensor H*, state seed s and operator seed s s^dagger. The theorem independently proves CK(Psi_U*)>=2 CS(Psi_U*). It is not inferred by replacing U* by I or by treating the two generators as identical. In the full-rank representation this is the vectorized sqrt(rho) flow, whose energy-difference measure is symmetric.

## Short-time structure

For a general state seed, center G by its expectation and write m_j=<G^j>, v=m_2>0. Direct Taylor/Lanczos algebra gives
\[
C_S=vt^2+\left(\frac{m_4}{6}-\frac{v^2}{2}-\frac{m_3^2}{4v}\right)t^4+O(t^6),
\]
\[
C_K=2vt^2+\left(\frac{m_4}{3}-v^2\right)t^4+O(t^6),
\qquad C_K-2C_S=\frac{m_3^2}{2v}t^4+O(t^6).
\]
The operator measure is the difference convolution of the state measure; its centered second and fourth moments are 2v and 2m4+6v^2. The v=0 case is exactly stationary and is handled without division.

For the I branch, v=Tr(rho H^2)-(Tr rho H)^2 and m3=Tr rho(H-Tr rho H)^3. For the U* branch, v=||[H,sqrt(rho)]||_HS^2 and every odd moment vanishes. The t^4 and t^6 gaps then vanish. In the regular moment/chain regime, putting w=m4 and u=m6, the first possible nonzero gap is
\[
C_K-2C_S=
\frac{(vu-6v^4+9v^2w-4w^2)^2}{72(w-v^2)(w+v^2)}t^8+O(t^{10}).
\]
This displayed coefficient is derived for nondegenerate moment recursions through the required order, with w>v^2; it is not used to assume away terminating chains. The two-point symmetric case w=v^2 has CK=2CS exactly, and all other terminated cases are covered directly by the flag theorem. No unverified limit substitution is needed for closure.

The coefficient 2 is sharp: for any nonstationary admissible finite-dimensional seed the short-time ratio tends to 2. A larger universal factor would already fail at sufficiently small times; balanced two-level pure seeds also give exact equality.

## Verification / kill phase

`verify_factor_two.py` verifies exact nested-projector identities for a state with a 3-dimensional state chain and a 7-dimensional joint chain inside a 9-dimensional tensor space, explicitly testing the incomplete-cyclic-space case. It compares full state-space and matrix-commutator evolution against a separate spectral-polynomial implementation in dimensions 2,3,4, for each purification branch and three times (18 branch/time checks). It checks the stronger tail inequalities, probability conservation, orthogonality, tridiagonality, exact physical subsystem evolution, complex common-unitary covariance, stationary seeds, zero gaps, repeated energies and two-level equality cases. An independent direct Taylor calculation confirms a nonzero quartic skewness gap. `derive_short_time.py` derives the symmetric moment series exactly.

Not implied: equality at all times, an equality/synchrony classification, the two-sided mixed-state hierarchy, a purity-only ratio law, arbitrary nonunitary dynamics, or validity of an undefined/incomplete Lanczos expansion. Independent external audit of this reconstruction has not been performed.
